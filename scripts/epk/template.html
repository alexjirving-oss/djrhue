About

Built for crowds that came to move

DJ RHUE is a Bristol-based selector with roots in Caribbean and urban nightlife. Sets are tailored in real time — warm-up grooves through to peak-time pressure — for clubs, festivals, private events and Malta stages.

Known for reading the room and delivering seamless, high-energy sets, he performs across the UK with international experience on festival stages in Malta. Technical mixing meets authentic Caribbean and African sounds to keep dancefloors moving all night.
